## Prevalence over the draws (design-weighted)

Per draw, from `reports/_draws/`. A respondent in stratum h counts N_h / r_h; the totals are weighted ratios over the strata with a weight. Gated: at least one gate the verification join keeps; in scope: such a gate with an in-scope condition. Intervals are 95% percentile bootstraps over whole campaigns within each stratum (2000 replicates, seed 20261024); a stratum with few names or no positives gives a degenerate interval. Non-response is left out of every denominator and listed by reason. The pilot is not a draw and is never in these figures.
